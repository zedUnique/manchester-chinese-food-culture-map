03 Food and culture

Cuisine, restaurants and signature dishes

Regional food tradition

Classified by evidenced culinary provenance: menu, restaurant self-description or fieldwork. Restaurants may carry more than one regional label; dish formats such as hotpot and malatang are not treated as regions.

Dietary needs

Recorded risks and explicitly identified vegetarian options only. Always confirm allergens, meat or seafood stock, cooking wine, halal status and cross-contact with the restaurant.

Research Notes

Sources and data notes

Restaurant and dish information is currently prototype data. Chinese-language sources are used to verify dish appearance and terminology. Third-party photographs are attributed and used only as academic visual references; obtain permission or replace them with fieldwork photographs before public deployment.
